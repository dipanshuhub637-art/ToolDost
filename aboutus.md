# ABOUT US

### Welcome to Tool Dost – Your Ultimate Everyday Utility Companion!

**Tool Dost** ek powerful, all-in-one utility application hai jise **Dipanshu Hub** ne digital life ko aasan aur efficient banane ke liye design kiya hai. Hamara mission ek aisa single platform provide karna hai jahan users ko daily life, business, aur technical works ke liye baar-baar alag-alag apps download na karni padein.

Aapke phone ka space bachane aur aapko ek seamless experience dene ke liye, Tool Dost apne andar **50+ smart aur essential tools** ka ek complete suite lekar aata hai.

---

### What Makes Us Special?

* **50+ Tools in One Place:** PDF editing, image compressing, aur unit conversion se lekar advanced tools jaise OCR document scanning, currency rates, aur text encryption tak—sab kuch ek hi app me maujood hai.
* **Privacy First (100% Secure & Offline):** Hamara manna hai ki aapka data sirf aapka hai. Tool Dost ke lagbhag sabhi tools completely offline aur client-side kaam karte hain. Hum aapka koi bhi personal data ya files kisi cloud server par store nahi karte.
* **No Login, No Hassle:** Hum user experience ko behtar banane ke liye registration ya complex sign-ups par zor nahi dete. App install kijiye aur turant use karna shuru kijiye.
* **Clean & Lightweight UI:** App ko is tarah design kiya gaya hai ki koi bhi ise bina kisi dikkat ke aasani se navigate aur use kar sake.

---

### Our Core Tool Categories:

1. **Smart Calculators:** GST, EMI, Age, Bill Splitter, aur Percentage calculators.
2. **Document & Image Handlers:** Image to PDF, PDF Merger, Image Compressor, aur Passport Resizer.
3. **Daily Utilities:** QR Scanner/Generator, Notepad, Voice Recorder, To-Do List, aur Translator.
4. **System & Device Tools:** Internet Speed Tester, Battery Health, Device Manager, aur Speaker Cleaner.
